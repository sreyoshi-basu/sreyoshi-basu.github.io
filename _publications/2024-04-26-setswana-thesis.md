M.A. in Linguistics, supervised by Prof. Joyce McDonough and Prof. Nadine Grimm. Describes how Setswana resolves vowel hiatus (glide formation, elision, diphthongization, glottal stop epenthesis) at morpheme, syllable and word boundaries. Presented at PHUNY 2024.
