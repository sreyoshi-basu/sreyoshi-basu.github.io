M.A. in English Language and Literature, supervised by Prof. S. Franklin Daniel.
Treats dance as a non-verbal language with both semantic and pragmatic layers. Beyond literal, codified gestures such as Bharatanatyam mudras, dance conveys implied meaning through body language, movement and facial expression. Drawing on Gricean pragmatics, the thesis argues that a pragmatic approach better captures how dance communicates meaning, illustrated through Rabindranath Tagore's satirical dance-drama *Tasher Desh* (The Land of Cards) and its theme of oppression versus freedom of expression.
